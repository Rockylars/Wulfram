**Pickup:** Soul of Oreb
<br>
**Text:** Two birds with one stone
<br>
**Appearance:** Colored purple, looks like a grape agate cluster crystal
<br>
**Clear rune:** 3 pips

- Switches all your health according to the following tables:
  - For normal characters:
    - full red heart <---> full soul heart
    - half red heart <---> half red bone heart
    - empty red heart <---> empty bone heart
    - full bone heart <---> full black heart
    - half soul heart <---> half black heart
    - rotten heart <---> rotten bone heart
    - bird skull <---> double crow skull
    - single crow skull --> bird skull
    - eternal heart --> golden heart
    - golden heart -> stays the same
  - For characters with only soul/black/bone hearts:
    - half soul heart <---> half black heart
    - full soul heart <---> full black heart
    - bone heart --> bird skull
    - bird skull --> double crow skull
    - single crow skull --> bone heart
    - double crow skull --> bone heart
    - eternal heart --> golden heart
    - golden heart -> stays the same
  - For characters with only black/bone hearts:
    - full black heart <---> double crow skull
    - half black heart <---> single crow skull
    - bone heart <---> bird skull
    - eternal heart --> golden heart
    - golden heart -> stays the same
  - For characters with only red/bone hearts
    - full red heart <---> full red bone heart
    - half red heart <---> half red bone heart
    - empty red heart <---> empty bone heart
    - rotten heart <---> rotten bone heart
    - bird skull <---> double crow skull
    - single crow skull --> bird skull
    - eternal heart --> golden heart
    - golden heart -> stays the same
  - For characters with no hearts or only coin hearts:
    - Does not swap health, instead grants a [Roaming Raven](/docs/items/passive/good/Roaming%20Raven/idea.md) and [Circling Crow](/docs/items/passive/good/Circling%20Crow/idea.md) for the floor.
